Questions people ask first.

Not covered here? Call or text on 0468 373 784.

How much will it cost?

Every yard is different, so you get a free quote first and a price before anything starts. Send a photo or have them come and look. No obligation either way.

Do you do regular visits or one-off jobs?

Both. Plenty of customers are on a regular mow, and just as many book a one-off tidy-up, a pruning job or a clean-up before an inspection.

What areas do you cover?

Bargara, Bundaberg and surrounding areas. If you are close by and not sure, just ask.

Do you take the green waste away?

Yes. Green waste removal is part of what they do, so the clippings, prunings and cleared beds go on the trailer and leave with them.

Can you handle a block that has been left a while?

Yes. Vacant blocks and acreage are on the list, and long grass, overgrown beds and palms that have got away are normal work rather than a special case.
