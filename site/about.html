The business

The people who actually turn up.

Leo and Chantel run Thomson & Foley themselves. They quote the job, they do the job, and they are the ones you call if anything needs sorting. Customers name them both in their reviews, which is what happens when the same two people keep coming back.

What they advertise is what they do: all lawn care and gardening services, with a high quality finish. Mowing, full garden tidy-ups, tree pruning, hedge trimming, green waste removals, mulch installs and light landscaping, plus vacant block and acreage mowing when a place has been left a while.

  • Quotes given on site, free and with no pressure
  • Communication before and during the job
  • Green waste loaded and taken away, not left in a pile
  • The job area left in good condition on completion
